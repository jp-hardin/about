Overview

Positioning Transportation & Logistics Companies for Maximum Value

Supply-chain reconfiguration has heightened buyer interest in logistics businesses with specialized capabilities and diversified customers. We advise asset-based and asset-light carriers, 3PLs, crating and rigging specialists and marine businesses, showing buyers the value of network density, customer relationships, safety records and technology.

Every engagement is led by the Mid-Market Practice and backed by Benchmark International's global network of strategic acquirers, private equity groups, family offices and independent sponsors.
