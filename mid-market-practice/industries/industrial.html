Overview

Industrial M&A Advisory

Reshoring, automation, infrastructure investment and the push for supply-chain resilience have put middle-market industrial companies at the top of many buyers' lists. Strategic acquirers want capabilities and capacity they can't build quickly. Private equity platforms want founder-built businesses with engineering know-how, sticky customers and room to grow through add-ons.

Our Industrial group advises owners of manufacturers, fabricators, distributors and industrial service providers across five segments. We translate machine capability, certifications, installed base and customer relationships into the terms buyers use to value them, then run a competitive process that puts those qualities in front of the right strategic and financial partners.

Every engagement is led by the BCM team and backed by Benchmark International's global network of strategic acquirers, private equity groups, family offices and independent sponsors.

Market Perspective

What We're Seeing in Industrial

01

Reshoring and supply-chain resilience

Customers are qualifying domestic suppliers and dual sources, which raises the value of certified capacity and long-standing OEM relationships.

02

Platform building by private equity

Sponsors keep assembling industrial platforms through add-on acquisitions, creating competitive tension for well-run founder-owned businesses.

03

Infrastructure and electrification

Spending on infrastructure, grid modernization and water is lifting demand for specialty contractors, power services and environmental providers.

04

Service and aftermarket revenue

Buyers pay a premium for recurring maintenance, repair and aftermarket revenue that smooths project and capital-spending cycles.

What Buyers Value

Seeing Your Company Through a Buyer's Eyes

We build the story around the factors that drive valuation in industrial, so qualified buyers compete on your terms.

  • 01

    Engineering know-how

    Proprietary processes, certifications such as AS9100 and ISO, and engineered-to-order capabilities.

  • 02

    Customer stickiness

    Approved-vendor status, long OEM relationships and recurring service agreements.

  • 03

    Capacity & equipment

    Modern equipment, documented maintenance and room for a buyer to add volume.
