Overview

Healthcare M&A Advisory

Healthcare remains one of the most resilient and actively consolidated sectors in the middle market. Demand is essential and growing, and health systems, public strategics and private equity platforms continue to acquire providers and suppliers to extend their reach.

Our Healthcare group represents physician and therapy practices, urgent care operators, laboratories, medical device and product companies, and pharmacy and healthcare services businesses. We show buyers the clinical quality, payer mix and growth behind each business while managing the regulatory and diligence complexity these transactions bring.

Every engagement is led by our Mid-Market team and backed by Benchmark International's global network of strategic acquirers, private equity groups, family offices and independent sponsors.

Market Perspective

What We're Seeing in Healthcare

01

Health system and payer consolidation

Hospital systems and national operators keep adding outpatient and urgent care sites to capture patients closer to home.

02

Outsourcing and specialization

Providers and pharma companies increasingly outsource labs, research, staffing and logistics to specialists.

03

Strategic interest in med-tech niches

Public strategics acquire focused device and product companies to add patented technology and clinical relationships.

04

Care moving to the home

Home infusion, home care and delivery services benefit as care shifts away from inpatient settings.
