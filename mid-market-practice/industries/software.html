Overview

Positioning Software Companies for Maximum Value

Vertical software with recurring revenue and high switching costs commands some of the strongest valuations in the market. We have represented pharmacy software, field-service, legal and compliance platforms in transactions with buyers ranging from software consolidators to large-cap private equity, and we know how to present ARR, retention, unit economics and the product roadmap.

Every engagement is led by the Mid-Market Practice and backed by Benchmark International's global network of strategic acquirers, private equity groups, family offices and independent sponsors.
