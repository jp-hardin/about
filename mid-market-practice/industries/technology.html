Overview

Technology M&A Advisory

Vertical software and mission-critical technology businesses command some of the strongest buyer interest in the market. Software consolidators, strategic acquirers and private equity firms compete for products that are deeply embedded in customer workflows, with recurring revenue and high switching costs.

Our Technology group has represented pharmacy software, field-service, lending, CAD/CAM and IT management platforms, as well as managed service providers and technology consultancies. We know how to present ARR, retention, unit economics and the product roadmap, and we bring buyers ranging from software holding companies to large-cap private equity.

Every engagement is led by our Mid-Market team and backed by Benchmark International's global network of strategic acquirers, private equity groups, family offices and independent sponsors.

Market Perspective

What We're Seeing in Technology

01

Software consolidators

Serial acquirers of vertical software are active buyers of founder-led companies with loyal niche customer bases.

02

Sponsor appetite for recurring revenue

Private equity continues to favor ARR, high net revenue retention and low churn.

03

AI and data

Buyers are looking for proprietary data and workflows that position a product to benefit from AI rather than be displaced by it.

04

Managed services and security

Demand for managed IT and cybersecurity keeps pulling platforms toward MSPs with contracted revenue and certified talent.

What Buyers Value

Seeing Your Company Through a Buyer's Eyes

We build the story around the factors that drive valuation in technology, so qualified buyers compete on your terms.

  • 01

    Recurring revenue

    ARR growth, net revenue retention and low churn.

  • 02

    Mission-critical product

    Deep workflow integration and high switching costs.

  • 03

    Scalable unit economics

    Efficient customer acquisition and a credible product roadmap.
