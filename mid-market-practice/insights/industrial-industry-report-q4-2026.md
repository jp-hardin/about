## 2. Market Conditions

### The Quarter in Brief

Demand spans the five segments, and buyers are acquiring through existing platforms. Higher financing costs in September reduced sponsors’ borrowing capacity.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR OWNERS

*Strategic acquirers and sponsor platforms are still buying machining, distribution, contracting, environmental, and logistics businesses for capabilities already in place.*

They are paying the most for companies tied to power, data centers, aerospace, and service work that codes and regulations require.

### Domestic manufacturing demand is broad

The ISM Manufacturing PMI was 54.5 in September, its ninth consecutive month of expansion, and orders for manufacturing technology reached $4.03 billion in the first seven months of the year, 37.1% ahead of 2025 ([ISM](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-54-5-september-2026-ism-manufacturing-pmi-report-302894520.html); [AMT data reported by MDM](https://www.mdm.com/news/research/economic-trends/u-s-metalworking-machinery-orders-slide-in-july/)). Manufacturing capacity utilization was 75.9% in September, so the scarce inputs are skilled labor and certified capacity rather than floor space ([Federal Reserve G.17](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/current/default.htm)).

### Private equity is buying through platforms it already owns

Add-on acquisitions made up about three-quarters of all buyouts in the second quarter ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.brightspotcdn.com/40/42/bbeb88094080abaea3d7c87530b8/q2-2026-us-pe-breakdown.pdf)), and Pye-Barker Fire & Safety completed 22 acquisitions in the first half of the year alone ([Pye-Barker](https://pyebarkerfs.com/?p=22122)). The sponsor at the table for a founder-owned industrial company is usually an operating platform that already knows its trade.

### Power and data center work is the strongest source of demand

Quanta Services ended June with backlog of $53.4 billion against $35.8 billion a year earlier ([Quanta Services](https://investors.quantaservices.com/_assets/_27cda7558592cc1119359235c3a11955/quantaservices/news/2026-07-30_QUANTA_SERVICES_REPORTS_SECOND_QUARTER_2026_402.pdf)). Public infrastructure funding is less certain, because the surface transportation authorization was extended only through December 11 under the continuing resolution signed in September ([AASHTO Journal](https://aashtojournal.transportation.org/?p=41360)).

### Recurring service revenue is valued above project revenue

APi Group is working toward 60% of revenue from inspection and service, and Transcat’s service revenue grew 27% ([APi Group](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251210396417/en/APi-Group-Announces-Acquisition-of-CertaSite-and-Provides-Full-Year-2025-Update); [Transcat](https://www.transcat.com/news/post/transcat-reports-strong-fiscal-first-quarter-2027-financial-results)). Buyers pay for service, repair, inspection, and aftermarket parts revenue when an owner reports it separately from project and product sales, with the renewal history behind it.

### Financing costs more and buyers are more selective

Global M&A value fell 41% from the second quarter to $993 billion in the third, and the Federal Reserve raised its target range to 3.75% to 4.00% on September 16 ([LSEG data reported by Reuters](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/global-m-deal-rush-fades-third-quarter-rising-borrowing/); [Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260916a.htm)). GF Data’s average multiple eased to 7.0 times trailing adjusted EBITDA from 7.3 times, and debt on platform acquisitions fell to 2.9 times from 3.4 times ([GF Data via ACG](https://www.acg.org/news-trends/news/gf-data-reports-show-steady-middle-market-deal-flow-amid-more-selective)). A sponsor that can borrow half a turn less bids less or moves more of the price into earn-outs; strategic buyers are affected less.

### Power Demand: How the Buildout Reaches the Industrial Segments

Electricity demand grows about 2% a year and the equipment is sold out years ahead, so acquirers buy the people, permits, and delivery slots already in place.

The Energy Information Administration expects US electricity sales to grow about 2% in 2026 and again in 2027, driven by data center development and commercial and industrial activity ([EIA](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/report/elec_coal_renew.php)). NERC raised its 10-year forecast of summer peak demand growth to 224 gigawatts from 132 ([POWER Magazine](https://www.powermag.com/nerc-warns-long-term-grid-reliability-risks-mounting/)), and Meta alone expects capital expenditures of $130 billion to $145 billion in 2026 ([Meta](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/meta-reports-second-quarter-2026-results-302838214.html)).

The equipment needed to meet that demand is committed years ahead. GE Vernova’s gas turbine backlog and slot reservations rose to 116 gigawatts from 100 during the second quarter ([GE Vernova](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001996810/000199681026000147/gevpressrelease2q26.htm)), and lead times for generator step-up transformers reached 160 weeks in the first quarter ([Reuters, citing Wood Mackenzie](https://www.ajot.com/news/us-power-companies-scramble-to-secure-equipment-as-surging-data-center-demand-strains-supplies)). Generac’s commercial and industrial sales grew 29% and its data center backlog reached about $1.6 billion ([Generac](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001474735/000143774926024731/ex_994826.htm)).

### Power is the constraint

> *“So, it’s interesting as they continue to announce all the data centers and data center sites. I’ve sort of always had the strong view that energy would be the constraint.”*

Greg Abel, CEO, Berkshire Hathaway (CNBC)

> *“Well, I don’t think it’s going to come down to chips or the models that people create or the apps that we develop. I think the consistent theme you hear here is can we get the power generation to power these massive data centers.”*

Toby Rice, CEO, EQT (EQT Corporation)

> *“Quite frankly, the biggest issue we are now having is not a compute glut, but it’s a power and it’s sort of the ability to get the builds done fast enough close to power. So, if you can’t do that, you may actually have a bunch of chips sitting in inventory that I can’t plug in.”*

Satya Nadella, CEO, Microsoft (Bg2 Pod)

## 3. Our View

### Our View

Five conclusions from the quarter, and what they mean for an owner planning a sale in 2027.

OUR VIEW IN ONE LINE

*Buyers are paying premiums for specialized capabilities. Higher borrowing costs affect the offers they can make.*

FOR AN OWNER GOING TO MARKET IN 2027

Report service, inspection, and aftermarket revenue separately from project and product sales, with renewal history.

Show backlog by end market and funded status, and name the technicians who will perform it.

Document how steel, diesel, and transformer cost increases pass through each contract.

01

### Specialized capabilities command higher prices

nVent paid 11.5 times for switchgear it could not build in time, Clean Harbors about 17 times standalone EBITDA for a route network, and Redwood and Imperative bought Laredo brokers for licenses and bonded space. A founder-owned company that holds a certification, a permit, a delivery slot, or a license that takes years to replicate is priced on that asset, at 8 to 12 times, against about 6 times for project and product capacity.

- 02 Higher rates affect sponsor financing

With the funds rate at 3.75% to 4.00%, the 10-year near 5%, and sponsor leverage at 2.9 times, a platform bid carries half a turn less debt than it did in the spring. Strategic acquirers with cash and synergies are affected less, and we expect their share of mid-market industrial sales, 56% of our closed transactions over two years, to rise through 2027. Sponsor offers will lean on earn-outs and seller notes, and the structure deserves as much attention as the headline number.

03

### Data center work supports listed contractors’ growth

Listed building products and construction companies grew revenue 22% while total construction spending fell 1.7% and manufacturing construction fell 19%. A mid-market contractor with funded data center backlog carries a premium, and one without it competes in a shrinking market for everything else. Limbach’s margin reset shows what happens when skilled labor is bid away, so buyers will underwrite the crews along with the backlog.

- 04 Recurring service revenue supports valuation

APi Group is steering toward 60% inspection and service revenue, Transcat’s service line grew 27%, and a project contractor like 1901 Inc. changed hands at 5.7 times. Buyers pay for revenue that renews under codes, regulations, and maintenance schedules, and they will diligence the split. An owner who reports service, repair, inspection, and parts separately, with renewal history, sells a different company from one who reports a single revenue line.

05

### Freight recovery and demand for border services

Cass shipments are up 2.1% off a two-year low, transportation shares rose a median 39% in a year, and C.H. Robinson agreed to pay $5.8 billion for RXO with $300 million of synergies behind it. Diesel at $6.29 a gallon is testing surcharge pass-through, and US-Mexico trade at a record $94.8 billion a month is why logistics platforms are buying Laredo brokers. Customs licensing, bonded warehousing, and cross-border capability will be priced above asset-based capacity through 2027.

### Buyers are paying for border capability

Trade with Mexico reached a monthly record of $94.8 billion in July, 27.5% above a year earlier, and $36.95 billion of it moved through Laredo ([Census data reported by FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/us-mexico-trade-smashes-monthly-record-at-nearly-95b)). Gonzalez, CHB](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/from-trucks-to-trade-compliance-logistics-firms-make-strategic-buys)).

Gaurang Shastri, Managing Director, Lincoln International, speaking to Transport Topics (Lincoln International)

### Manufacturing PMI® at 54.5%; September 2026 ISM® Manufacturing PMI® Report

> *“The New Orders Index expanded for the ninth consecutive month after four straight readings in contraction.”*

Susan Spence, Chair, ISM Manufacturing Business Survey Committee

The September PMI reached 54.5%, and new orders have grown nine months running after four months of contraction, so an owner planning a sale should show two or three quarters of order growth in the monthly financials before going to market.

### GF Data Reports Show Steady Middle-Market Deal Flow Amid More Selective Financing Conditions in Q2

> *“Average total debt on platform transactions declined to 2.9x TTM EBITDA from 3.4x in Q1.”*

GF Data Leverage Report

Average purchase multiples slipped to 7.0x from 7.3x across 85 completed deals in the second quarter while senior debt pricing rose to 7.8%, so sellers should expect price discipline and use a quality of earnings report to defend a premium.

[READ HERE](https://www.acg.org/news-trends/news/gf-data-reports-show-steady-middle-market-deal-flow-amid-more-selective)

REUTERS (VIA KITCO NEWS)  |  9 JULY 2026

### US power companies scramble to secure equipment as surging data center demand strains supplies

> *“Equipment availability is becoming the biggest concern for developers as they value time to market so highly.”*

Ben Boucher, senior analyst, Wood Mackenzie

Generator step-up transformer lead times passed 160 weeks against 143 weeks on average in 2024, and utilities now buy equipment for projects they expect five years ahead, so owners serving grid and data center work should document backlog, supplier agreements, and delivery slots before launching a process.

[READ HERE](https://www.kitco.com/news/off-the-wire/2026-07-09/us-power-companies-scramble-secure-equipment-surging-data-center)

FREIGHTWAVES  |  7 OCTOBER 2026

### Mexico remains top US trading partner as August commerce hits $94.3B

> *“Imports through Laredo totaled $26.23 billion, a 33.9% increase, while exports increased 18.3% to $12.34 billion.”*

Noi Mahoney, FreightWaves (reporting Census trade data)

August trade between the US and Mexico reached $94.3 billion and Laredo two-way trade rose 28.5%, so owners with cross-border exposure should document customer mix, tariff pass-through terms, and lane concentration, which buyers test in diligence.
