## 1. The Short Version

American manufacturing has expanded for nine consecutive months, and acquirers in every industrial sector are buying capacity that already exists because skilled labor is scarce and new equipment takes a long time to arrive. Borrowing costs rose in September, and buyers have become more selective about what they pay for.

**KEY INSIGHTS**

**01  Power and data center work earns the premium**

Quanta Services ended June with $53.4 billion of backlog, up from $35.8 billion. A buyer will ask how much of yours is tied to funded power and data center work.

**02  The likely buyer is an operating platform**

Add-ons made up about three-quarters of buyouts in the second quarter, and Pye-Barker Fire & Safety completed 22 acquisitions in the first half. Expect a sponsor that already knows the trade.

**03  Report service revenue on its own line**

APi Group is working toward 60% of revenue from inspection and service. Buyers pay more for service, repair and aftermarket revenue when it is reported apart from projects, with its renewal history.

**04  Financing costs more and buyers are choosier**

The Federal Reserve raised its target range to 3.75% to 4.00% on September 16. GF Data's average multiple eased to 7.0 times trailing adjusted EBITDA, from 7.3 times.

## 2. Market Conditions

### Market Insights: Q4 2026

Demand is broad, buyers are organized around platforms and financing became more expensive in September.

WHAT IT MEANS FOR OWNERS

*Strategic acquirers and private equity platforms are still buying machining, distribution, contracting, environmental and logistics capacity that already exists.*

They are paying the most for companies tied to power, data centers, aerospace and service work that codes and regulations require.

### Domestic manufacturing demand is broad

The ISM Manufacturing PMI was 54.5 in September, its ninth consecutive month of expansion, and orders for manufacturing technology reached $4.03 billion in the first seven months of the year, 37.1% ahead of 2025 ([ISM](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/manufacturing-pmi-at-54-5-september-2026-ism-manufacturing-pmi-report-302894520.html); [AMT data reported by MDM](https://www.mdm.com/news/research/economic-trends/u-s-metalworking-machinery-orders-slide-in-july/)). Capacity utilization of 75.7% leaves most plants room to take on more work ([Federal Reserve G.17](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g17/current/default.htm)).

### Private equity is buying through platforms it already owns

Add-on acquisitions made up about three-quarters of all buyouts in the second quarter ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.brightspotcdn.com/40/42/bbeb88094080abaea3d7c87530b8/q2-2026-us-pe-breakdown.pdf)), and Pye-Barker Fire & Safety completed 22 acquisitions in the first half of the year alone ([Pye-Barker](https://pyebarkerfs.com/?p=22122)). The sponsor at the table for a founder-owned industrial company is usually an operating platform that already knows its trade.

### Power and data center work is the strongest source of demand

Quanta Services ended June with a backlog of $53.4 billion against $35.8 billion a year earlier ([Quanta Services](https://investors.quantaservices.com/_assets/_27cda7558592cc1119359235c3a11955/quantaservices/news/2026-07-30_QUANTA_SERVICES_REPORTS_SECOND_QUARTER_2026_402.pdf)). Public infrastructure funding is less certain, because the surface transportation authorization expired on September 30 and highway programs are running under a continuing resolution through December 11 ([AASHTO Journal](https://aashtojournal.transportation.org/?p=41360)).

### Recurring service revenue is valued above project revenue

APi Group is working toward 60% of revenue from inspection and service, and Transcat’s service revenue grew 27% ([APi Group](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251210396417/en/APi-Group-Announces-Acquisition-of-CertaSite-and-Provides-Full-Year-2025-Update); [Transcat](https://www.transcat.com/news/post/transcat-reports-strong-fiscal-first-quarter-2027-financial-results)). Buyers pay more for service, repair, inspection and aftermarket parts revenue when an owner reports it separately from project and product sales, with the renewal history behind it.

### Financing costs more and buyers are more selective

Global M&A value fell 41% from the second quarter to $993 billion in the third, and the Federal Reserve raised its target range to 3.75% to 4.00% on September 16 ([LSEG data reported by Reuters](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/global-m-deal-rush-fades-third-quarter-rising-borrowing/); [Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260916a.htm)). GF Data’s average multiple eased to 7.0 times trailing adjusted EBITDA from 7.3 times, and debt on platform acquisitions fell to 2.9 times from 3.4 times ([GF Data via ACG](https://www.acg.org/news-trends/news/gf-data-reports-show-steady-middle-market-deal-flow-amid-more-selective)).

### Power Demand: The Buildout Reaches Every Industrial Sector

Electricity demand is growing about 2% a year, the equipment to meet it is sold out years ahead and acquirers are buying existing capacity.

The Energy Information Administration expects US electricity sales to grow about 2% in 2026 and again in 2027, driven by data center development and commercial and industrial activity ([EIA](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/report/elec_coal_renew.php)). NERC raised its 10-year forecast of summer peak demand growth to 224 gigawatts from 132 ([POWER Magazine](https://www.powermag.com/nerc-warns-long-term-grid-reliability-risks-mounting/)), and Meta alone expects capital expenditures of $130 billion to $145 billion in 2026 ([Meta](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/meta-reports-second-quarter-2026-results-302838214.html)).

The equipment needed to meet that demand is committed years ahead. GE Vernova’s gas turbine backlog and slot reservations rose to 116 gigawatts from 100 during the second quarter ([GE Vernova](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001996810/000199681026000147/gevpressrelease2q26.htm)), and lead times for generator step-up transformers reached 160 weeks in the first quarter ([Reuters, citing Wood Mackenzie](https://www.ajot.com/news/us-power-companies-scramble-to-secure-equipment-as-surging-data-center-demand-strains-supplies)). Generac’s commercial and industrial sales grew 29% and its data center backlog reached about $1.6 billion ([Generac](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001474735/000143774926024731/ex_994826.htm)).

### AI Bottleneck Is Power, Not Chips

> “So, it’s interesting as they continue to announce all the data centers and data center sites. I’ve sort of always had a strong view that energy would be the constraint.”

Greg Abel, CEO, Berkshire Hathaway (CNBC)

> “Well, I don’t think it's going to come down to chips or the models that people create or the apps that we develop. I think the consistent theme you hear here is can we get the power generation to power these massive data centers.”

Toby Rice, CEO, EQT (EQT Corporation)

> “quite frankly, the biggest issue we are now having is not a compute glut, but it’s a power and it's sort of the ability to get the builds done fast enough close to power. So, if you can't do that, you may actually have a bunch of chips sitting in inventory that I can’t plug in.”
