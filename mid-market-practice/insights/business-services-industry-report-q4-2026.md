## 1. Industry at a Glance

**KEY INSIGHTS**

**01  Inspection work draws the deepest buyer pool**

Pye-Barker completed 22 acquisitions in the first half of 2026, after 57 in 2025. Acquirers compete hardest for contracted inspection and service work.

**02  Costlier debt puts a premium on proof**

Debt on platform acquisitions fell to 2.9x EBITDA from 3.4x, and the Federal Reserve raised its target range on September 16. Documented earnings and contracted customers now command the premium.

**03  Engineering buyers pay for people and backlog**

Bernhard Capital agreed to buy Bowman at a 58% premium, and 88% of engineering firms report open positions. Licensed staff and a year of funded backlog set the price.

**04  Prepared sellers still draw several offers**

Completed middle-market deals averaged 7.0x EBITDA in the second quarter, and 87% of sales above $5 million drew three or more offers. Keep every client below a fifth of revenue.

## 2. Market Conditions

Six developments are shaping demand, pricing, and deal activity across the five sectors as the fourth quarter begins.

### Inspection-driven revenue draws the most active buyers

**22**

Pye-Barker Fire & Safety completed 22 acquisitions in the first half of 2026 after 57 in 2025 ([Pye-Barker](https://pyebarkerfs.com/news/pye-barker-fire-safety-celebrates-mid-year-momentum-and-3-sdm-100-ranking/)). APi Group grew 10.1% organically in the second quarter and reported a backlog above $5 billion ([APi Group](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001796209/000162828026050806/apg-20260630xexx991.htm)).

*Acquirers compete most actively for contracted inspection and service work.*

### Financing became more expensive in the third quarter

**4.99%**

The Federal Reserve raised its target range to 3.75% to 4.00% on September 16 ([Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/files/monetary20260916a1.pdf)), and the 10-year Treasury yield averaged 4.99% in September ([FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/GS10)). Debt on platform acquisitions fell to 2.9x EBITDA from 3.4x ([GF Data via ACG](https://www.acg.org/news-trends/news/gf-data-reports-show-steady-middle-market-deal-flow-amid-more-selective)).

*Costlier debt puts a premium on documented earnings and contracted customers.*

### Engineering backlogs run a year deep and licensed staff are scarce

**88%**

The median engineering firm holds 12 months of backlog, and 88% of firms report open positions ([ACEC](https://engineeringinc.acec.org/blog/engineering-firms-remain-resilient-amid-growing-economic-uncertainty-new-acec-research-institute-study-finds/)). Bernhard Capital agreed to buy Bowman for about $1.0 billion of enterprise value, a 58% premium ([Bowman](https://investors.bowman.com/news-releases/news-release-details/bowman-consulting-group-enters-definitive-agreement-be-acquired)).

*Buyers of engineering firms are paying for licensed staff and funded backlog.*

### Softer insurance pricing has slowed broker organic growth

**−2%**

Commercial premiums fell 2% on average in the second quarter, with property down 6.3% ([Insurance Journal](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2026/08/20/882240.htm)). Aon agreed to buy USI for $17 billion in cash ([Insurance Journal, September 21](https://www.insurancejournal.com/magazines/mag-business-moves/2026/09/21/885520.htm)).

*Small commercial and casualty accounts are seeing the least pricing pressure.*

### Staffing revenue is growing for the first time in three years

**+5%**

Median staffing revenue grew 5% in August, the highest reading since late 2022 ([Staffing Industry Analysts](https://www.staffingindustry.com/editorial/it-staffing-report/it-staffing-revenue-up-5-at-the-median-in-pulse-survey)). Temporary help employment remains 21% below its March 2022 peak ([BLS via FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/TEMPHELPS)).

*Skilled trades, engineering, and healthcare placements are drawing acquirers.*

### Advertising is growing while AI changes what agencies sell

**+9.6%**

US advertising revenue is forecast to grow 9.6% in 2026 ([MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/417751/ad-outlook-composite-improves-on-mw-revisions.html)). Marketing budgets hold at 7.8% of company revenue, with 15.3% of those budgets directed to AI ([Marketing Dive](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/ai-remains-top-priority-cmos-102100803.html)).

*Retained clients, first-party data, and measurable outcomes hold their value.*

WHAT IT MEANS
FOR OWNERS

Buyers pay most readily for revenue that repeats under contract. Completed middle-market transactions averaged 7.0x EBITDA in the second quarter ([GF Data via ACG](https://www.acg.org/news-trends/news/gf-data-reports-show-steady-middle-market-deal-flow-amid-more-selective)), the median multiple on sales of $5 million to $50 million reached 5.8x ([IBBA and M&A Source](https://www.ibba.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/mp-highlights-q2-2026.pdf)), and 87% of sales above $5 million drew three or more offers ([IBBA and M&A Source release](https://www.webull.com/news/15462423712777216)).

Figures are the latest published as of October 7, 2026, and each is linked to its source. The 10-year Treasury figure is the monthly average for September 2026. EBITDA is earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization.

### Fire and Life Safety

Inspection and service revenue explains why fire protection and security companies have the most active acquirers in business services.

INDUSTRY FEATURE

### Why fire and life safety has the most active acquirers

Pye-Barker alone bought 79 fire and security companies in the 18 months to June 2026, and APi Group, Sciens Building Solutions, and AI Fire are buying alongside it. Three features of the work explain the interest.

1

**The work is contracted or mandated**

Inspection, testing, and monitoring repeat on a schedule, so the revenue continues when new construction slows.

2

**Each acquired branch adds density**

A buyer that already runs routes in a region can serve an acquired book of accounts with the technicians it has.

3

**Inspection leads the customer relationship**

APi bought CertaSite, with about $90 million of revenue, because the business leads with inspections and earns repair and service work from them.

### Engineering firms hold a year of work and expect a slower economy

The third-quarter survey of engineering firms recorded a median backlog of 12 months, with the strongest sentiment in data centers and in water and wastewater. The same firms put the odds of a recession within six months at 41%, and 88% reported open positions.

**Our read.** Backlog and a bench of licensed engineers are the first two facts a buyer will test.

### Staffing revenue grows 5% at the median, the best reading since 2022

The August Pulse Survey of 128 staffing firms recorded median revenue growth of 5% from a year earlier. Engineering staffing led at 10% and industrial staffing followed at 8%, after three years in which industry revenue declined.

**Our read.** The recovery is strongest in the skilled categories where this year's acquisitions are concentrated.

### Commercial premiums fall 2% as property pricing softens

The Council of Insurance Agents & Brokers recorded an average premium decline of 2% in the second quarter. Large accounts fell 3.7% and small accounts 0.5%. Commercial property fell 6.3% while umbrella and commercial auto continued to rise.

**Our read.** Account size and line of business now explain most of the difference in agency growth.
