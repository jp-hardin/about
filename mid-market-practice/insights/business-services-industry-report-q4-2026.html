The Quarter in Brief

Business services companies enter the fourth quarter of 2026 with acquirers active in all five segments covered in this report and with financing more expensive than at any point this year. Global deal value fell 41% in the third quarter after a record first half, following a Federal Reserve rate increase in September and a 10-year Treasury yield above 5%. The measures closest to founder- and family-owned companies moved the other way: the median multiple on transactions of $5 million to $50 million reached 5.8x in the second quarter, the highest since early 2022, and 87% of transactions above $5 million drew three or more offers.

02

Deal Market

Deal market: a record first half, then a rate shock in the third quarter

Global announced M&A reached $2.8 trillion in the first half of 2026, up 48% on the prior year and the highest first-half total since LSEG began keeping records in 1980, although the number of deals fell 9% to roughly 24,000, a six-year low (Reuters, LSEG data, July 1, 2026). The third quarter reversed the trend in value: global volume fell 41% from the second quarter to $993 billion, the first quarter under $1 trillion since mid-2025, and Reuters attributed the drop to rising borrowing costs (Reuters, October 1, 2026). North America recorded 10,230 transactions worth $2.37 trillion through nine months, up 33%, with third-quarter value of $560.4 billion running 23% below the same quarter of 2025 (Mergermarket, October 1, 2026).

Borrowing costs rose sharply over the same months, as the Federal Reserve raised its target range by 25 basis points to 3.75% to 4.00% on September 16, 2026, stating that inflation remains elevated (Federal Reserve, FOMC statement). Consumer prices rose 3.4% over the twelve months to August, with energy up 16.3% (BLS, CPI), and the 10-year Treasury yield moved above 5% in mid-September (Vanguard, September 17, 2026). The underlying economy is still growing: real GDP rose at a 2.2% annual rate in the second quarter (BEA), unemployment stood at 4.2% in September with payrolls up 29,000 (BLS, Employment Situation, October 2, 2026), and banks reported commercial lending standards basically unchanged in the second quarter (Federal Reserve, Senior Loan Officer Opinion Survey, July 2026).

Private equity carries a large inventory of companies it needs to sell and a large pool of capital it needs to invest, and both conditions favor a well-prepared private seller. Bain counted 32,000 unsold portfolio companies worth $3.8 trillion at the end of 2025, almost 40% of them held for more than five years, alongside $1.3 trillion of buyout dry powder (Bain & Company, Global Private Equity Report 2026). US private equity deal value fell to $177.3 billion in the second quarter of 2026, down 37.5% from the first quarter, and add-on acquisitions made up about three-quarters of the buyout transactions completed in the quarter (PitchBook, Q2 2026 US PE Breakdown). PwC estimates that clearing the existing private equity inventory at the current pace would take nine years (PwC, US Deals 2026 Midyear Outlook). A purchase by a platform the sponsor already owns is smaller and easier to finance at current rates than a new platform investment, which is why sponsor-backed companies appear as the buyer in so many of the transactions in this report.

McKinsey expects about six million small and mid-sized US businesses to change hands by 2035, and more than half of small-business owners are now over 55 (McKinsey, The great ownership transfer).

Federal tax law is settled for sellers planning a 2026 or 2027 closing. The top long-term capital gains rate remains 20%, and the estate and gift exemption stands at $15 million per person, indexed, under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act of July 2025 (Tax Foundation). The same law raised the qualified small business stock exclusion to $15 million for stock issued after July 4, 2025, restored 100% bonus depreciation and made the 20% pass-through deduction permanent (HBK CPAs & Consultants).

Commercial and facility services hold the most active acquirers in business services, because the work is contracted or mandated, it repeats on a route or an inspection schedule, and each acquired branch adds density to a network the buyer already operates. Cintas, which serves more than one million business customers over approximately 12,500 local delivery routes, agreed in March 2026 to acquire UniFirst for about $5.5 billion, and UniFirst's shareholders have approved the transaction (Cintas, fiscal 2026 annual report; American Laundry News, July 6, 2026).

Fire protection and life safety

APi Group reported second-quarter 2026 revenue of $2,254 million, up 13.3% and 10.1% organically, with an adjusted EBITDA margin of 13.8% and a backlog above $5 billion (APi Group, July 30, 2026). APi described CertaSite, a Midwest provider with about $90 million of revenue that it agreed to buy in December 2025, as an "inspection-first" business that moves it toward earning more than 60% of revenue from inspection and service (APi Group, December 10, 2025). Pye-Barker Fire & Safety completed 57 acquisitions in 2025 and a further 22 in the first half of 2026, more than 100 in two years, across fire alarm, sprinkler, suppression and security alarm companies (Security Systems News, March 18, 2026; Pye-Barker, July 30, 2026).

Security services

The SDM 100 ranking of electronic security dealers recorded recurring monthly revenue of $740 million for 2025, up 7%, with 90% of ranked dealers growing that revenue and 76% operating in commercial accounts (SDM Magazine, May 4, 2026). In guard services, Allied Universal closed eight acquisitions between May and July 2026 with about $205 million of combined annual revenue (Allied Universal, September 22, 2026), while Securitas reported flat organic sales in the second quarter (Securitas, July 24, 2026). The Bureau of Labor Statistics counts 1.3 million security guards at a median wage of $18.29 an hour, which makes the wage escalation terms in a guard company's customer contracts a central diligence item (BLS).

Painting and property maintenance

ABM Industries grew revenue 4.2% to $2.3 billion in its fiscal third quarter, with 2.1% organic growth and an equal contribution from acquisitions (ABM, September 8, 2026). FirstService Residential grew 4% on new contract wins (FirstService, July 23, 2026), and BrightView's maintenance revenue rose 1.8% while its adjusted EBITDA fell 15.1% (BrightView, August 4, 2026). Regional platforms are doing much of the buying: 4M Building Solutions has made 16 janitorial acquisitions since January 2023 (Business Wire, July 21, 2026), and Landscape Workshop acquired three commercial landscape maintenance businesses in August 2026 to reach more than 50 locations in 16 states (Utah Business, August 27, 2026). Labor is the common constraint, with 2.4 million janitors and building cleaners earning a median $17.71 an hour (BLS).

Managed print and document services

Equipment demand is contracting while the independent dealer channel continues to grow revenue. IDC reported worldwide hardcopy peripheral shipments of 17.2 million units in the second quarter of 2026, down 7.0% year on year (IDC, September 8, 2026). The Cannata Report's 40th Annual Dealer Survey, drawn from 415 dealerships, found that 67% of dealers grew revenue, by 14% on average (The Cannata Report, October 30, 2025), that 47% of dealers now offer managed IT services, and that 73% of those dealers grew the line, by an average of 28.8% (The Cannata Report, managed IT findings). Dan Doyle Jr., chief executive of DEX Imaging, told the publication that dealers need to commit fully to IT, with print representatives supplying the leads and technical specialists closing the sale (The Cannata Report, September 18, 2026).

The buyer pool is concentrated in a small number of acquirers that are moving quickly. DEX Imaging, owned by Staples, completed its sixth acquisition of 2026 in September, UBEO Business Services has announced four, and Pacific Office Automation has closed three, including its entry into Houston, and 45% of surveyed dealers said they planned to acquire a dealership or service company (The Cannata Report, acquisition trends). The manufacturers are consolidating as well: Xerox bought Lexmark for $1.5 billion in 2025 (Pulse 2.0, July 30, 2026), and Ricoh, Toshiba Tec and OKI now manufacture their multifunction devices through one joint venture, ETRIA (Ricoh, February 13, 2025). A dealer's value therefore rests more on its customer contracts, service organization and IT revenue than on the brand it carries, and the terms of its dealer agreements on a change of ownership deserve an early reading. In records and information management, Iron Mountain reported second-quarter 2026 revenue of $2.0 billion, up 18.5% (IT Brief, August 7, 2026).

Testing, inspection and certification

The largest listed companies are growing and acquiring at the same time. SGS reported first-half 2026 sales of CHF 3,683 million, up 7.6%, with organic growth of 5.6% and 14 bolt-on acquisitions completed in 2026 by the date of the release, and it closed its purchase of Applied Technical Services, a North American testing, inspection, calibration and forensics provider, on January 12, 2026 (SGS, July 23, 2026). Bureau Veritas reported first-half organic growth of 5.0% and raised its 2026 guidance to mid- to high-single-digit organic growth (Quartr, Bureau Veritas first-half 2026 results), and UL Solutions grew second-quarter revenue 6.6% organically with an adjusted EBITDA margin of 26.8% (UL Solutions, August 4, 2026).

Private equity is committing capital to testing at every size. In June 2026 the board of Intertek, the UK-listed testing group, unanimously recommended an offer from a company controlled by EQT of £60 in cash per share plus a final dividend of 107.7 pence, which values Intertek at an enterprise value of £10.9 billion and represents a 62% premium to the closing price on April 9, 2026 (Business News Today, June 18, 2026). KPMG records the transaction at $14.6 billion and places it among the largest global private equity deals of the second quarter (KPMG, Pulse of Private Equity Q2 2026). In the United States, TIC Solutions, which was formed in August 2025 when Acuren completed its acquisition of NV5 Global, announced on August 18, 2026 that it had completed three further acquisitions with about $90 million of combined annualized revenue (TIC Solutions, August 18, 2026), and Transcat paid $84 million in cash for Essco Calibration Laboratory in August 2025 (Transcat, August 5, 2025).

Buyers in testing and inspection pay for accreditation, mandated recurring revenue and certified staff. Accreditation and licensure take years to obtain and limit the number of firms that can perform a given test or inspection. Revenue recurs because the work is required by a regulator, an insurer or a customer's quality system, which makes it less sensitive to the economy than project work. Certified technicians and inspectors are scarce, so an acquisition is often the fastest way for a larger group to add capacity in a new region.

Advertising demand is running well ahead of the wider economy in 2026. WPP Media's midyear forecast has global advertising revenue growing 8.9% this year to $1.3 trillion excluding US political spending, with the United States up 11.9% (LBB Online, June 15, 2026). MediaPost's composite of the major forecasters puts US growth at 9.6% in 2026 including political advertising and 4.4% in 2027, when the midterm and World Cup lift falls away (MediaPost, September 9, 2026). US internet advertising revenue reached $294.6 billion in 2025, up 13.9%, with commerce media up 18% to $63.4 billion (IAB and PwC, April 16, 2026). Client budgets are steadier than the media totals suggest: Gartner's 2026 survey of 401 chief marketing officers found marketing budgets at 7.8% of company revenue, against 7.7% a year earlier, with 15.3% of those budgets directed to AI (Marketing Dive, May 11, 2026).

The holding companies are directing their own capital toward data and technology assets, and the announced mid-market transactions of the past twelve months cluster in four areas: agencies specialized in one vertical such as healthcare, legal or home services; firms with proprietary audience data or retail media technology; experiential and event businesses; and promotional products and print distributors joining larger platforms.

Outsourced sales and brokerage

Advantage Solutions, the largest listed sales and marketing agency for consumer packaged goods brands, grew second-quarter 2026 revenue 1.8% to $889.5 million while its adjusted EBITDA fell 12.2%. Revenue in its Branded Services segment fell 20.1%, which management attributed to constrained client spending, insourcing and client losses, and its Experiential segment grew 19.7% (Advantage Solutions, August 5, 2026). Buyers of independent brokers and manufacturers' representatives therefore look closely at the length of each principal relationship and at how much revenue rests on any one brand.

Promotional products

ASI measured North American distributor sales at a record $27.7 billion in 2025, up 4.2%, with nearly 90% of distributors raising prices, by about 11% on average, to offset import costs, and with distributors above $5 million of revenue growing 9.3% (ASI, January 21, 2026). Growth slowed to 1.8% in the first quarter of 2026 and 2.5% in the second, with firms under $1 million of revenue flat and larger firms growing about 4% (ASI Central, July 14, 2026). 4imprint's first-half revenue rose 1% while its operating profit fell 16% on tariff-related supplier costs (4imprint, August 5, 2026), and PPAI's economist described 2025 as a year in which many distributors sold more and made less (PPAI).

Digital strategy and consulting

Gartner forecasts worldwide IT services spending of $1.57 trillion in 2026, up 5.3% after 3.8% growth in 2025 (Gartner, July 27, 2026). Accenture reported fiscal 2026 revenue of $74.2 billion, up 6%, and spent $4.9 billion on acquisitions during the year (Accenture, October 1, 2026), and Cognizant grew second-quarter revenue 4.5% and completed its $634 million acquisition of Astreya (Cognizant, July 29, 2026).

Education technology consulting

The E-Rate program that funds school and library connectivity has a cap of $5.2 billion for the funding year that began on July 1, 2026, against estimated demand of $3.5 billion, and the FCC directed that all eligible requests be funded (FCC, May 11, 2026). The Supreme Court upheld the Universal Service Fund's contribution mechanism in June 2025 (SCOTUSblog), and the FCC ended E-Rate support for hotspot lending and school bus Wi-Fi that September (American Library Association). CoSN's 2026 survey of district technology leaders ranks cybersecurity as the top priority for the eighth year, with 65% reporting inadequate cybersecurity staffing (CoSN via K-12 Dive, May 14, 2026).

Display and signage

Signs of the Times found that 48% of sign companies grew sales in 2025, down from 70% in 2023, that 72% expect growth in 2026, and that recruiting and retaining staff is the leading threat they name (Signs of the Times, March 17, 2026). Daktronics reported sales up 7.1% and a backlog of $311.3 million in its quarter ended August 1, 2026, with commercial orders up 5.3% and live events orders down 48.8% (Daktronics, September 2, 2026), and the exhibition industry's index is forecast to grow 2.1% in 2026 (IAEE, May 4, 2026).

Artificial intelligence is changing what a marketing firm is paid for. The groups under the most pressure are reducing headcount while growth concentrates in technology-led work, and WPP Media sizes generative search advertising at $5.1 billion globally in 2026 (exchange4media, June 16, 2026). Agencies whose revenue depends on producing content or search rankings by the hour should expect buyers to examine how much of that work clients can now do themselves, and agencies with retained client relationships, first-party data and measurable outcomes should expect that examination to work in their favor.

Engineering firms enter the fourth quarter with full order books and a cautious outlook. The ACEC Research Institute's third-quarter 2026 survey recorded a median backlog of 12 months, with nearly half of firms holding a year or more of work and the strongest sector sentiment in data centers and in water and wastewater, while the same firms put the odds of a recession within six months at 41% (ACEC, August 27, 2026). ENR's 2026 ranking put the combined revenue of the 500 largest design firms at $158.7 billion, up 7.4% (ENR, April 27, 2026). Architecture is weaker: the AIA/Deltek Architecture Billings Index stood at 47.2 in August 2026, below the level of 50 that separates growth from contraction (AIA, September 23, 2026).

Federal surface transportation funding is the principal uncertainty for civil and transportation firms. The Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act expired on September 30, 2026, and the continuing resolution that runs through December 11 extends the core highway and transit programs without the law's advance appropriations of about $36.8 billion a year (Holland & Knight, August 12, 2026). NACo estimates that the extension reduces overall funding by more than 15% and eliminates the Bridge Formula Program (NACo, September 2, 2026). Firms whose backlog depends on federally funded bridge, rail and safety grants should expect buyers to ask how much of that work is already obligated.

The listed firms show where demand is strongest. Stantec's backlog reached $9.2 billion, up 17.5%, and its water business grew 13.0% organically in the first half, although organic growth in the United States was flat in the second quarter (Stantec, August 12, 2026). Tetra Tech's backlog rose to $4.49 billion (Tetra Tech, July 29, 2026), Willdan's net revenue grew 23.5% with 18% organic growth (Willdan, August 6, 2026), and TIC Solutions reported a record backlog of $1.18 billion in consulting, engineering and geospatial work (TIC Solutions, August 6, 2026).

Surveying, geospatial and forensic engineering

Specialist practices are selling to consolidators built around a single discipline. Commercial insurance pricing turned negative in 2026, and the effect on an agency depends on the accounts and lines it writes. The Council of Insurance Agents & Brokers recorded an average premium decline of 2% in the second quarter, with large accounts down 3.7%, medium accounts down 1.9% and small accounts down 0.5%, and with commercial property down 6.3% while umbrella rose 5.3% and commercial auto rose 4.5% (Insurance Journal, August 20, 2026). An agency weighted toward small commercial accounts and casualty lines is therefore seeing less pressure on commissions than one weighted toward large property accounts.

Organic growth at the listed brokers has slowed with pricing, and acquisitions account for most of the growth they report. Second-quarter organic growth was 7% in WTW's broking business, 6% at Gallagher, about 5% at Aon and 3% in Marsh's risk and insurance business (Insurance Business, July 31, 2026), and 6.7% at Ryan Specialty (Ryan Specialty, July 30, 2026). Claims adjusting

Revenue at independent adjusting firms rises and falls with weather claims, and 2026 has brought fewer of them. Staffing revenue is growing again after three years of decline. Staffing Industry Analysts' August 2026 Pulse Survey recorded median year-on-year revenue growth of 5% across 128 firms, the highest reading since late 2022, led by engineering at 10% and industrial at 8% (SIA, September 29, 2026). Preparing for a Sale

Sellers who prepare early capture more of the competition for their business

In the second quarter of 2026, 87% of transactions above $5 million attracted three or more offers, and those sales took 11 to 12 months from engagement to closing, yet most sellers had spent less than a year preparing (IBBA and M&A Source Market Pulse). An owner who expects to sell within three years should therefore have reviewed financial statements, a clear record of customer contracts and renewals, and a second layer of management in place before the first buyer conversation.

Higher interest rates make this work more valuable, because lenders are advancing less debt against each dollar of EBITDA and buyers are reserving their strongest offers for businesses whose earnings they can verify quickly. The same preparation helps the accountants, attorneys and wealth advisors who counsel an owner, since tax, estate and reinvestment planning each take longer than the sale process itself.
