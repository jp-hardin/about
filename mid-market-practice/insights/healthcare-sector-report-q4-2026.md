## 2. Insights

**KEY INSIGHTS**

**01  Strategic buyers are setting the pace**

Private equity is on pace for 674 healthcare services transactions this year, the slowest since 2017. Medtech deal value reached $36.5 billion in the first half. Put strategic acquirers on every buyer list.

**02  Debt costs healthcare buyers more**

Direct lenders widened buyout loan spreads to an average of 509 basis points in the second quarter, from 474 in the first. Senior debt on completed middle-market deals was priced at 7.8%.

**03  Recurring revenue earns the best price**

The median private equity entry multiple for healthcare services was 13.6x EBITDA in 2025, weighted toward larger platforms. The best prices go to revenue that recurs under contract or clinical necessity, paid by a balanced mix of payers.

**04  Plan now for 2027 rate and notice rules**

Medicare proposes physician conversion factor cuts of up to 1.68% for 2027. Maine's 180-day notice and approval regime begins January 1, 2027. Model the rates and build review time into the deal calendar.

## 3. Healthcare M&A: State of Play

Strategic buyers are setting the pace in 2026 while private equity completes fewer healthcare services transactions.

### M&A market conditions

Private equity completed an estimated 337 healthcare services transactions in the first half, a pace of 674 for the year and the slowest since 2017 ([PitchBook via Fierce Healthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/finance/projected-healthcare-services-deal-count-be-lowest-2017-pitchbook-finds)).

Strategic buyers carried more of the activity. Medtech deal value reached $36.5 billion in the first half ([PwC via MedTech Dive](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/medtech-ma-maintains-momentum-following-decade-high-2025-pwc/823113/)) and health systems announced 18 transactions in the second quarter ([Kaufman Hall](https://www.kaufmanhall.com/news/hospital-and-health-system-ma-activity-among-highest-q2-levels-2019)).

Biopharma deal value reached $130 billion in six months, almost matching the total for all of 2025 ([IQVIA](https://www.iqvia.com/locations/emea/blogs/2026/07/biopharma-ma-mid-year-2026-update)).

### Reimbursement and policy

Medicare has proposed 2027 physician conversion factors of $33.17 and $32.84, reductions of 1.19% and 1.68%, with the final rule pending ([CMS](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/calendar-year-cy-2027-medicare-physician-fee-schedule-proposed-rule)).

Hospital outpatient, surgery center and home health rates are each proposed to rise 2.4% ([CMS outpatient rule](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/prospective-payment-systems/hospital-outpatient/regulations-notices); [CMS home health](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/calendar-year-cy-2027-home-health-prospective-payment-system-proposed-rule-fact-sheet-cms-1844-p)), and Medicare Advantage rates rise 2.48% ([CMS rate notice](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/2027-medicare-advantage-part-d-rate-announcement)).

Medicaid work requirements and six-month eligibility reviews for expansion adults begin in 2027 ([Medicaid.gov](https://www.medicaid.gov/resources-for-states/working-families-tax-cut-legislation)).

### Financing and macro backdrop

The Federal Reserve raised its target range to 3.75% to 4.00% on September 16 ([Federal Reserve](https://federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260916a.htm)) and the 10-year Treasury yielded 5.24% on October 1 ([US Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202610)).

Direct lenders widened buyout loan spreads to an average of 509 basis points in the second quarter from 474 in the first ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.brightspotcdn.com/40/42/bbeb88094080abaea3d7c87530b8/q2-2026-us-pe-breakdown.pdf)).

Completed middle-market transactions in all industries averaged 7.0x EBITDA, with senior debt priced at 7.8% ([GF Data via ACG](https://www.acg.org/news-trends/news/gf-data-reports-show-steady-middle-market-deal-flow-amid-more-selective)).

### Demand fundamentals

National health spending grew 6.8% in the year to July 2026 and represented 18.4% of GDP ([Altarum](https://www.altarum.org/news-and-insights/september-2026-health-sector-economic-indicators-briefs)).

CMS projects nearly $9.0 trillion of spending in 2034, or 20.6% of GDP, on average annual growth of 5.4% ([CMS](https://www.cms.gov/data-research/statistics-trends-and-reports/national-health-expenditure-data/nhe-fact-sheet)).

Healthcare employers added 17,000 jobs in September against a twelve-month average of 33,000 ([Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_10022026.htm)).

### Trade and deal regulation

A 100% default tariff applies to covered patented pharmaceuticals, with country and company exceptions and generics excluded ([White House proclamation](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/04/adjusting-imports-of-pharmaceuticals-and-pharmaceutical-ingredients-into-the-united-states/)). The medical device investigation remained open in September with no tariff imposed ([iData Research](https://idataresearch.com/news-medical-device-tariffs-in-2026/)).

California now requires transaction notices from private equity groups and management services organizations ([Nixon Peabody](https://www.nixonpeabody.com/insights/alerts/2026/05/27/ohcas-proposed-emergency-regulations-clarify-ab-1415-notice-requirements)), and Maine's 180-day notice and approval regime begins January 1, 2027 ([BakerHostetler](https://www.bakerlaw.com/insights/healthcare-and-private-equity-transactions-continue-to-be-under-state-scrutiny/)).

**WHAT IT MEANS FOR OWNERS**

Buyers are paying most readily for revenue that recurs under contract or clinical necessity and that is paid by a balanced mix of payers.

The median private equity entry multiple for healthcare services was 13.6x EBITDA in 2025, a figure weighted toward larger platforms ([PitchBook via Becker's ASC Review](https://www.beckersasc.com/asc-transactions-and-valuation-issues/why-asc-deals-are-outperforming-the-rest-of-healthcare-ma-in-2026-8-things-to-know/)), and 87% of sales above $5 million in all industries drew three or more offers ([IBBA and M&A Source](https://www.webull.com/news/15462423712777216)).

Figures are the latest published as of October 7, 2026, and each is linked to its source. Deal counts and values are as reported by the named publisher and are revised as transactions are confirmed.

## 4. Historical Market Context

Health spending has compounded through every cycle since 2016, and hospital transaction counts have recovered from the 2025 low.

*Spending on care has grown every year for a decade, while the number of transactions has moved with financing costs and policy.*

**$5.3T**

National health expenditures reached $5.3 trillion in 2024, or 18.0% of GDP, against $3.3 trillion in 2016 ([CMS](https://www.cms.gov/data-research/statistics-trends-and-reports/national-health-expenditure-data/nhe-fact-sheet)).

**5.4%**

CMS projects average annual spending growth of 5.4% from 2025 to 2034, against 4.1% for the economy, which would take spending to $9.0 trillion ([Committee for a Responsible Federal Budget](https://www.crfb.org/blogs/national-health-expenditures-continue-rise)).

**46**

Hospitals and health systems announced 46 transactions in 2025, the fewest in the series, against 117 in 2017 ([Kaufman Hall](https://www.kaufmanhall.com/news/hospital-and-health-system-ma-activity-ends-2025-momentum)).

**40**

The first half of 2026 has already produced 40 announced hospital transactions, 22 in the first quarter and 18 in the second ([Kaufman Hall, first quarter](https://www.kaufmanhall.com/sites/default/files/2026-04/KH-Report_M%2BA_2026-Q1.pdf); [second quarter](https://www.kaufmanhall.com/news/hospital-and-health-system-ma-activity-among-highest-q2-levels-2019)).

**1,217**

Private equity completed 1,217 healthcare services transactions in 2021 and an estimated 747 in 2025 ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.brightspotcdn.com/2d/0a/acb3601c4e19925c22d3f3877637/q4-2025-healthcare-services-report.pdf)).

## 10. Healthcare Services Deal Volume Has Reset

Private equity completed 1,217 healthcare services transactions in 2021 and is on pace for 674 in 2026, the fewest since 2017.

2017 to 2019

### Steady platform building

Private equity completed between 490 and 684 healthcare services transactions a year, and hospitals announced 117 transactions in 2017 and 92 in 2019.

2020 to 2021

### The peak

Volume rose to 829 in 2020 and 1,217 in 2021, the highest in the series.

2022 to 2024

### Three years of decline

Annual volume fell from 998 to 682. The Medicare conversion factor fell from $34.61 in 2022 to $32.35 in 2025 ([AMA](https://www.ama-assn.org/system/files/2021-01/cf-history.pdf)).

2025 to 2026

### Strategic buyers lead

Volume recovered to an estimated 747 in 2025. The first half of 2026 produced an estimated 337, while medtech deal value reached $36.5 billion and biopharma deal value reached $130 billion.
