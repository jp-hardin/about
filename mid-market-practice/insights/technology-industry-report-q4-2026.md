## 2. Market Conditions

### Key Trends

Six developments are shaping demand, pricing, and deal activity as the fourth quarter begins.

- **AI infrastructure sets the pace of demand.** Gartner expects IT spending of $6.37 trillion in 2026, up 14.2%, with data center systems up 62.5% and IT services up 5.3% (Gartner). Cloud infrastructure revenue grew 43% in the second quarter (Synergy Research Group).
- Celestica, TD SYNNEX, and Wesco each reported double-digit growth on data center demand in the latest quarter (page 22).
- **Consolidators keep buying recurring revenue.** Buyers announced 64 acquisitions of managed service providers in the first quarter, 73% more than a year earlier, and outside investors led 80% of them. Evergreen Services Group alone completed 47 acquisitions in 2025 (Omdia).
- Evergreen, Thrive, Integris, and The 20 MSP are each absorbing several providers a year, and contracted managed revenue is what they underwrite.
- **Financing became more expensive in the third quarter.** The Federal Reserve raised its target range to 3.75% to 4.00% on September 16 (Federal Reserve) and the 10-year Treasury yielded 5.24% on October 1 (US Treasury). Platform acquisition debt fell to 2.9x EBITDA from 3.4x (GF Data via ACG).
- At 2.9x debt and a 7.0x average price, sponsors are funding about 60% of a purchase with equity, which favors sellers with contracted revenue.
- **Security budgets grow while the defense mandate pauses.** Gartner's July 2025 forecast puts 2026 security spending at $240 billion, up 12.5% (Gartner); cybercrime losses reached $20.9 billion in 2025 (FBI). The Department of War suspended CMMC third-party certification on July 13 (Department of War CIO).

### Tariffs, Copper, and Financing: Costs Buyers Are Repricing

Tariffs, component and copper prices and interest rates have each moved in the past year and change what buyers treat as normal earnings.

A technology company that reaches the market in the next year will present earnings that passed through three tariff regimes, a 27% rise in component prices, a record copper price, and the first increase in the federal funds rate since 2023.

The effect differs by business. Manufacturers and distributors that import components carry duty exposure, integrators that fix a price on cabling and power work carry copper exposure, and managed service firms with contracted revenue carry mostly financing exposure.

- An owner who can show landed cost by period, copper recovery by contract, and a clear account of how the business is financed shortens diligence and protects the earnings figure the price is built on.
- **Tariffs and components.** The Supreme Court held on February 20 that the International Emergency Economic Powers Act does not authorize tariffs, a 10% Section 122 tariff ran until it expired on July 24, and Section 301 tariffs of 10% to 12.5% took effect that day.
- **What buyers ask for.** A bill of materials by country of origin with duties paid since February 2025, the status of any refund claim, and the contract terms that pass tariffs through to customers.
- **Copper and power.** Copper averaged $13,543 a ton in July, up 38.6% from a year earlier, and reached a record $14,875 a ton on the London Metal Exchange on September 10.
- **What buyers ask for.** Contracted and spot pricing for copper-bearing cable and switchgear on backlog projects, the escalation clauses that apply, and the margin effect of a 10% move in copper.
- **Financing.** The Federal Reserve raised its target range to 3.75% to 4.00% on September 16, and the 10-year Treasury yielded 5.24% on October 1.
- **What buyers ask for.** How much of the price is paid in cash at closing, how much is contingent, and how much is rolled into the buyer’s equity, and how the company’s own debt is structured.

## 3. Our View

Buyers favor contracted revenue and backlog that converts to cash.

**About 60%**

of a typical sponsor purchase is funded with equity, at 2.9x debt against a 7.0x average price ([GF Data via ACG](https://www.acg.org/news-trends/news/gf-data-reports-show-steady-middle-market-deal-flow-amid-more-selective)).

**87%**

of sales above $5 million drew three or more offers ([IBBA and M&A Source](https://www.ibba.org/resource-center/market-pulse-reports/)).

**7 of 11**

named sellers in our recent technology transactions were bought by a company in their own or an adjacent field (page 13).

- **Our position.** Buyers pay full prices for contracted revenue, essential capabilities, and backlog that converts to cash. Lower debt availability makes them more selective elsewhere.
- **Our advice: to owners.** Document contracted revenue by customer, term, and renewal history; equity-funded buyers price what they can verify.
- Add pass-through or escalation terms to open backlog before a process, with component and copper prices up more than 25%.
- Approach strategic buyers in adjacent fields alongside sponsors; seven of our eleven named sellers sold to one.

## 5. Deals and Buyers

### Recent Transactions

Ten of the twelve transactions carry a disclosed price, six carry a value to revenue multiple, and two carry a multiple of earnings.

Softcat, a UK reseller, agreed in September to acquire General Datatech, a Dallas multi-vendor IT solutions provider owned by H.I.G.

Accenture agreed in June and completed on September 21 a majority investment in the industrial security firm Dragos and the purchase of runZero and NetRise, which together had about $208 million of annual recurring revenue in June, up 53% (SecurityWeek; Constellation Research).

- Proofpoint, owned by Thoma Bravo, agreed on October 15 to acquire Varonis, a public data security and access governance software company, for $49.75 per share in cash, a 52% premium to its June 22 closing price.
- TDS Telecom agreed in April and completed on September 1 the purchase of Granite State, a New Hampshire fiber and voice provider whose more than 11,000 fiber service addresses sit next to the TDS footprint (TDS Telecom; Light Reading).
- BRC Group Holdings agreed on September 28 to acquire Sangoma, a Markham, Ontario provider of business communications software with more than 100,000 business customers and about $200 million of trailing revenue, for cash and BRC shares worth US$5.225 per Sangoma share (Business Wire).
- Public acquirers disclosed prices for the larger deals, while sponsor-backed platforms and regional operators kept terms private.
- Persistent Systems, which held 21%, launched a voluntary cash offer on June 28 of EUR 81 per share for Nagarro, a listed digital engineering and IT services group, a 140% premium to the undisturbed price.
- Motorola Solutions agreed in June and completed on August 20 the purchase of D-Fend, an Israeli counter-drone company whose radio frequency cyber-takeover technology is deployed in more than 30 countries and which expected about $185 million of revenue in 2026 (Motorola Solutions).
- TTM Technologies agreed on October 19 to buy the Ophir Optics business from MKS, a maker of precision infrared optics with plants in Israel and Romania, for about $650 million in cash.

## 7. Indicators to Watch

Component and copper prices turned up in 2026, data center construction is surging, and IT services employment keeps drifting lower.

- **Semiconductor and electronic component prices.** Producer price index, semiconductor, and other electronic component manufacturing. Index, September 2021 = 100.
- **Change to Aug 2026.** Component prices were flat for four years and have risen sharply since February 2026, which raises input costs for manufacturers and integrators.
- **Copper price.** Global price of copper, indexed from the monthly average in US dollars per metric ton. Index, September 2021 = 100.
- **Change to Jul 2026.** Copper feeds cabling, power, and fiber construction budgets, and it reached a record monthly average in 2026.
- **Private office construction, including data centers.** Annualized spending; the Census Bureau counts data centers in this category. Index, September 2021 = 100.
- **Change to Aug 2026.** Data centers accounted for $85.0 billion of the August 2026 annual rate, against $49.1 billion a year earlier.
- **Employment in computer systems design and related services.** All employees, seasonally adjusted. Index, September 2021 = 100.
