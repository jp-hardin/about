## 2. Market Conditions

### State of Play

Strategic buyers are setting the pace in 2026 while private equity completes fewer healthcare services transactions.

### M&A market dynamics

Private equity completed an estimated 337 healthcare services transactions in the first half, a pace of 674 for the year and the lowest annual count since 2017 ([PitchBook via Fierce Healthcare](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/finance/projected-healthcare-services-deal-count-be-lowest-2017-pitchbook-finds)).

Strategic buyers carried more of the activity: medtech deal value reached $36.5 billion in the first half ([PwC via MedTech Dive](https://www.medtechdive.com/news/medtech-ma-maintains-momentum-following-decade-high-2025-pwc/823113/)), and hospitals and health systems announced 56 transactions through September, 16 of them in the third quarter ([Kaufman Hall](https://www.kaufmanhall.com/news/ma-activity-remains-strong-among-large-health-systems-amid-continued-industry-transformation)).

Biopharma deal value reached $130 billion in six months, almost matching the total for all of 2025 ([IQVIA](https://www.iqvia.com/locations/emea/blogs/2026/07/biopharma-ma-mid-year-2026-update)).

### Reimbursement and policy

Medicare has proposed 2027 physician conversion factors of $33.17 and $32.84, reductions of 1.19% and 1.68%, with the final rule pending ([CMS](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/calendar-year-cy-2027-medicare-physician-fee-schedule-proposed-rule)).

Hospital outpatient, surgery center, and home health rates are proposed to rise 2.4% ([CMS outpatient rule](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/calendar-year-2027-hospital-outpatient-prospective-payment-system-opps-ambulatory-surgical-center); [CMS home health](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/calendar-year-cy-2027-home-health-prospective-payment-system-proposed-rule-fact-sheet-cms-1844-p)), and Medicare Advantage rates rise 2.48% ([CMS rate notice](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/2027-medicare-advantage-part-d-rate-announcement)).

Medicaid work requirements and six-month eligibility reviews for expansion adults begin in January 2027 ([CMS](https://www.medicaid.gov/medicaid/downloads/elig-redet-section-71107-deck.pdf)).

### Financing and macro backdrop

The Federal Reserve raised its target range to 3.75% to 4.00% on September 16 ([Federal Reserve](https://federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260916a.htm)), and the 10-year Treasury yielded 5.09% on October 16 ([US Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=202610)).

In a small sample of recent buyout loans, PitchBook found direct-lender spreads averaging 509 basis points in the second quarter against 474 in the first ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.brightspotcdn.com/40/42/bbeb88094080abaea3d7c87530b8/q2-2026-us-pe-breakdown.pdf)).

Completed middle-market transactions across industries averaged 7.0x EBITDA, with senior debt priced at 7.8% ([GF Data via ACG](https://www.acg.org/news-trends/news/gf-data-reports-show-steady-middle-market-deal-flow-amid-more-selective)).

FOR OWNERS

Medicaid redeterminations begin in January, so a Medicaid-weighted provider should expect diligence on enrollment attrition. Negotiated Medicare drug prices cut Guardian Pharmacy’s growth to 2% in one quarter. Operating companies are winning auctions against sponsors because senior debt prices at 7.8%.

Private equity paid a median 13.6x EBITDA for healthcare services companies in 2025, on targets with median revenue of $162.5 million ([PitchBook](https://www.beckersasc.com/asc-transactions-and-valuation-issues/why-asc-deals-are-outperforming-the-rest-of-healthcare-ma-in-2026-8-things-to-know/)[via Becker’s ASC Review](https://www.beckersasc.com/asc-transactions-and-valuation-issues/why-asc-deals-are-outperforming-the-rest-of-healthcare-ma-in-2026-8-things-to-know/)), and 87% of sales above $5 million across industries drew three or more offers ([IBBA and M&A Source](https://www.ibba.org/market-pulse-q2-2026-survey-results/)).

### Demand fundamentals

National health spending grew 6.8% in the year to July 2026 and represented 18.4% of GDP ([Altarum](https://www.altarum.org/news-and-insights/september-2026-health-sector-economic-indicators-briefs)).

CMS projects nearly $9.0 trillion of spending in 2034, or 20.6% of GDP, on average annual growth of 5.4% ([CMS](https://www.cms.gov/data-research/statistics-trends-and-reports/national-health-expenditure-data/nhe-fact-sheet)).

Healthcare employers added 17,000 jobs in September against a twelve-month average of 33,000 ([Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_10022026.htm)).

### Trade and deal regulation

A 100% default tariff applies to covered patented pharmaceuticals, with country and company exceptions and generics excluded ([White House proclamation](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/04/adjusting-imports-of-pharmaceuticals-and-pharmaceutical-ingredients-into-the-united-states/)). The medical device investigation remained open in September with no tariff imposed ([iData Research](https://idataresearch.com/news-medical-device-tariffs-in-2026/)).

California now requires transaction notices from private equity groups and management services organizations ([Nixon Peabody](https://www.nixonpeabody.com/insights/alerts/2026/05/27/ohcas-proposed-emergency-regulations-clarify-ab-1415-notice-requirements)), and Maine’s 180-day notice and approval regime begins January 1, 2027 ([Foley & Lardner](https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2026/04/maine-health-care-transactions-new-state-approvals-involving-some-private-equity-hedge-funds-and-msos/)).

Figures are as of October 16, 2026, and deal counts and values are as reported by the named publisher and are revised as transactions are confirmed.

Health spending has grown steadily since 2016, and hospital transaction counts have recovered from the 2025 low.

*Spending on care has grown annually for a decade, and transaction counts have moved with financing costs and policy.*

The first three quarters of 2026 produced 56 announced hospital transactions: 22 in the first quarter, 18 in the second, and 16 in the third ([Kaufman Hall](https://www.kaufmanhall.com/news/ma-activity-remains-strong-among-large-health-systems-amid-continued-industry-transformation)).

## 3. Our View

Strategic buyers hold the advantage into 2027, and the owners who do best will have answered the policy questions before a buyer asks them.

THE HOUSE VIEW

Operating companies and the largest platforms are buying through 7.8% senior debt and 500-basis-point spreads because they pay from cash flow, and hospital transactions are tracking to their most active year since 2020 while private equity’s healthcare services count is at a 2017 pace.

We expect that division to hold through the first half of 2027. Sponsor volume returns once the September rate increase is absorbed and the 2027 Medicare and Medicaid rules are final, and the 2021 vintage of platforms still waiting for an exit will bring supply to market before then.

For an owner, the next twelve months favor a process built around strategic buyers, launched with the payer-mix and enrollment analysis already done and timed ahead of the sponsor exits that will crowd the second half of 2027.

- 01 Strategic Buyers

Operating companies bought seven of the nine named sellers in Benchmark’s last 24 months of healthcare closings, HCA has added about 70 urgent care centers this year, and Danaher, Merck KGaA, and Labcorp are paying for consumables and testing volume. A buyer list that leads with strategics and brings in sponsors for competitive tension suits a 2027 process.

- 02 Reimbursement Dates

The 2027 physician fee schedule proposes conversion factor cuts of 1.2% to 1.7%, Medicaid work requirements, and six-month redeterminations start in January, and laboratory fee cuts of up to 15% a year resume unless the RESULTS Act passes. Buyers will model these items line by line, so an owner who brings that analysis to the first meeting stays in control of the valuation conversation.

- 03 Adjusted Multiples

On company-reported adjusted EBITDA the listed reference set trades at 14.3x, with provider services at 14.2x, medical products at 10.7x and pharmacy services at 15.0x, all within about a turn of a year ago, while life sciences and diagnostics re-rated to 18.7x. Private healthcare services deals cleared a 13.6x median in 2025 on targets with $162.5 million of revenue, against 5.8x for $5 million to $50 million businesses across industries. Scale, contracted revenue, and payer mix account for the gap between those two numbers.

- 04 Supply

PitchBook’s healthcare analyst expects mean reversion from the 2026 low, and AMN and Thermo Fisher both described fuller pipelines on their second-quarter calls. Sponsor exits are running 26% below last year’s pace, which builds a backlog of platforms waiting to sell. Owners who launch in the first half of 2027 meet buyers before that backlog clears.

MEDICAL PRODUCTS & DISTRIBUTION

*Procedure demand is firm and consumables are outgrowing capital equipment.*

SUBSECTORS WE COVER

KEY TRENDS
