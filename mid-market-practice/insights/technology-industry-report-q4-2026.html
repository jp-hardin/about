Insights

Part 1  ·  Key trends, perspectives on six sectors and what we are reading

Key insights

  1. Data center demand outpaces IT services

    Gartner expects data center systems spending to rise 62.5% in 2026, against 5.3% for IT services. Firms tied to data center build-outs can show buyers faster growth.

  2. Managed revenue brings funded bidders

    Buyers announced 64 acquisitions of managed service providers in the first quarter, 73% more than a year earlier. Contracted managed revenue can put several funded bidders in one process.

  3. Dearer debt favors documented earnings

    Total debt on platform acquisitions fell to 2.9x EBITDA from 3.4x, and the Federal Reserve raised its target range on September 16. Documented earnings and contracted customers carry more weight.

  4. Backlog counts once it ships

    North American electronics manufacturing services bookings rose 53.5% in August, while shipments rose 1.0%. Show that backlog converts at steady margins. Expect questions on component supply.

01

Key Trends in Technology

Six developments are shaping demand, pricing and deal activity across the six sectors as the fourth quarter begins.

AI infrastructure sets the pace of demand
+62.5%

Gartner expects IT spending of $6.37 trillion in 2026, up 14.2%, with data center systems up 62.5% and IT services up 5.3% (Gartner). Cloud infrastructure revenue grew 43% in the second quarter (Synergy Research Group).

Firms tied to data center build-outs are outgrowing IT services as a whole.

Consolidators keep buying recurring revenue
64

Buyers announced 64 acquisitions of managed service providers in the first quarter, 73% more than a year earlier, and outside investors led 80% of them. Evergreen Services Group alone completed 47 acquisitions in 2025 (Omdia).

Contracted managed revenue draws several funded buyers to a provider.

Financing became more expensive in the third quarter

5.24%

The Federal Reserve raised its target range to 3.75% to 4.00% on September 16 (Federal Reserve) and the 10-year Treasury yielded 5.24% on October 1 (US Treasury). Total debt on platform acquisitions fell to 2.9x EBITDA from 3.4x (GF Data via ACG).

Dearer debt puts a premium on documented earnings and contracted customers.

Security budgets grow while the defense mandate pauses

$240B

Gartner forecasts information security spending of $240 billion in 2026, up 12.5% (Gartner), after reported cybercrime losses of $20.9 billion in 2025 (FBI). The Department of War suspended the third-party certification phase of CMMC on July 13 (DoD CIO).

Revenue tied to one compliance mandate needs a second source of demand.

Hardware order books are filling faster than factories ship

1.39

North American EMS bookings rose 53.5% in August while shipments rose 1.0%, a three-month book-to-bill ratio of 1.39 (Global Electronics Association). Section 301 duties replaced the expired 10% import surcharge on July 24 (Wiley Rein).

Backlog earns its value when it converts to shipments at steady margins.

Federal technology budgets rise as award timing slips

$75.7B

The fiscal 2027 budget requests $75.7 billion for civilian agency IT against $67.9 billion in fiscal 2026 (FedScoop). A continuing resolution funds the government through December 11, which delays new program starts (SpacePolicyOnline).

Recompete dates and set-aside status deserve attention before a sale process.

WHAT IT MEANS
FOR OWNERS

Buyers pay most readily for contracted, recurring revenue and for capability that customers or regulators require. Completed middle-market transactions averaged 7.0x EBITDA in the second quarter (GF Data via ACG), and 87% of sales above $5 million drew three or more offers (IBBA and M&A Source).

Figures are the latest published as of October 5, 2026 and each is linked to its source. EMS is electronics manufacturing services. CMMC is the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification for defense suppliers.
