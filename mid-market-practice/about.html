Industries We Cover

  • Industrial: Manufacturing, industrial distribution, building products, energy and environmental services, and transportation.

  • Consumer: Food and beverage, consumer brands and products, foodservice and distribution, and consumer services.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Benchmark International's Middle Market team do?

Benchmark International's Middle Market team represents privately held companies across the United States in sell-side M&A, recapitalizations and growth partnerships.

What size companies does the Middle Market team advise?

The team serves privately held companies with a total enterprise value (TEV) of $50 million to $500 million.

Which industries does the Middle Market team focus on?

Five industry groups: Industrial, Business Services, Consumer, Healthcare and Technology.

What services does the Middle Market team provide?

Services include sell-side m&a, recapitalizations & partial sales, strategic partnerships & growth capital, exit planning & valuation.

How does working with Benchmark International start?

The first step is always a confidential conversation about your goals, your company and your options. A senior member of the Middle Market team sets up a private conversation, then walks you through the paths available, from a partial recapitalization to a full sale.
