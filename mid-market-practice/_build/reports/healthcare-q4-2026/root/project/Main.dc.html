HEALTHCARE

Q4 2026 M&A Sector Report
October 2026 - Jared Hardin | Managing Director | Benchmark International

The quarter in brief

Healthcare enters the fourth quarter of 2026 with health systems, distributors and public strategic buyers setting the pace, and with private equity completing fewer and more carefully underwritten transactions. Private equity closed roughly 337 healthcare services deals in the first half, a pace that would make 2026 the slowest year since 2017, while medtech deal value reached $36.5 billion over the same six months and pharma and life sciences recorded their strongest quarter since 2020. Demand underneath the deal market is steady, with national health spending running 6.8% above last year in July and projected to reach nearly $9.0 trillion by 2034.
