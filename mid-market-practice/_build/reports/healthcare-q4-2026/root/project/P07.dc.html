The reimbursement calendar bears on price in each of these trades. Medicare's proposed 2027 dialysis rule would raise total payments 1.1% (CMS), and Acadia Healthcare, whose adjusted EBITDA fell to $149.2 million from $201.8 million, has named Medicaid work requirements and labor costs as risks to behavioral health volumes (Acadia Healthcare). Buyers are paying most readily for in-network commercial revenue, dense local footprints and clinicians who stay after closing.
