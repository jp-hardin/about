Private capital is paying for licensed talent and funded backlog. Bowman Consulting Group, a founder-led firm with more than 2,500 employees whose backlog grew 50.3% to $658.7 million (Bowman, August 10, 2026), agreed in August 2026 to be acquired by Bernhard Capital Partners for $43.00 per share, which is about $1.0 billion of enterprise value and a 58% premium to the prior closing price (Bowman, August 10, 2026). Ardurra, owned by Littlejohn & Co., made five acquisitions in 2025 and reached 2,600 employees with its purchase of the 500-person Remington & Vernick Engineers (ENR, March 10, 2026), and PSMJ counted 447 announced transactions among architecture, engineering and construction firms in 2025 (PSMJ). The constraint these buyers are solving is people: 88% of engineering firms report open positions and 96% have roles that require a professional engineering license (ACEC, August 27, 2026), the average vacancy rate is 7% (ACEC, March 4, 2026), and the Bureau of Labor Statistics projects about 22,700 openings for civil engineers each year (BLS).
