BUSINESS SERVICES · Q4 2026

What buyers reward and what they discount

Across the five segments, acquirers pay the most for revenue that repeats under contract and discount revenue that depends on the owner, on one customer or on work that technology is making cheaper. The specifics differ by segment, and an owner or advisor can test a business against them well before a sale.

Segment
Buyers reward
Buyers discount
Commercial & Facility Services
Inspection, monitoring and service contracts that renew each year; route density within a region; licenses and accreditation held by the company; managed IT revenue alongside print; a tenured technician bench
Revenue concentrated in installation projects or equipment sales; licenses held personally by the owner; dependence on one end market or one manufacturer; equipment in need of reinvestment
Marketing, Sales & Tech-Enabled Services
Retained client and principal relationships; depth in one vertical such as healthcare, legal or home services; proprietary data or technology; a management team beyond the founder
Project revenue that restarts each year; one client or brand above a fifth of revenue; hourly content or search work that clients can now produce with AI tools; product margin exposed to import costs
Engineering, Architecture & Consulting
Funded backlog of a year or more; licensed staff and a second generation of principals; public-sector and utility relationships; exposure to water, power and data center work
Ownership and client relationships concentrated in principals near retirement; backlog that depends on federal grants not yet obligated; a history of project write-offs or professional liability claims
Financial & Insurance Services
Recurring commissions and fees with high retention; commercial and specialty books; producers and advisers under contract beyond the owner; clean carrier and compliance records
Books weighted toward lines where premiums are falling; revenue tied to catastrophe claims or refinancing volume; producers or advisers who control their client relationships personally
Staffing & Human Capital
Specialty placements in skilled trades, engineering, healthcare and technology; gross margin held through the downturn; direct client relationships; recruiter tenure
Commodity light industrial and clerical volume; heavy dependence on one managed service provider program; workers' compensation claims history; reliance on visa-dependent talent

Structure carries part of the price in many of these transactions. Among the private company acquisitions that closed in 2025, 24% included an earnout and 21% left management with retained equity (SRS Acquiom 2026 Deal Terms Study via Fasken), which means that the qualities buyers reward affect the terms an owner is offered as well as the headline multiple.

Acquirers pay the most for revenue that repeats under contract, in every one of the five segments.
