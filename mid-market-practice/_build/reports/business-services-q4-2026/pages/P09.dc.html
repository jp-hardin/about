Advertising demand is running well ahead of the wider economy in 2026. WPP Media's midyear forecast has global advertising revenue growing 8.9% this year to $1.3 trillion excluding US political spending, with the United States up 11.9% (LBB Online, June 15, 2026). MediaPost's composite of the major forecasters puts US growth at 9.6% in 2026 including political advertising and 4.4% in 2027, when the midterm and World Cup lift falls away (MediaPost, September 9, 2026). US internet advertising revenue reached $294.6 billion in 2025, up 13.9%, with commerce media up 18% to $63.4 billion (IAB and PwC, April 16, 2026). Client budgets are steadier than the media totals suggest: Gartner's 2026 survey of 401 chief marketing officers found marketing budgets at 7.8% of company revenue, against 7.7% a year earlier, with 15.3% of those budgets directed to AI (Marketing Dive, May 11, 2026).

Omnicom's integration of Interpublic removed more than 4,000 positions and folded FCB, DDB and MullenLowe into other networks (Strategy, December 1, 2025). The holding companies are directing their own capital toward data and technology assets, and the announced mid-market transactions of the past twelve months cluster in four areas: agencies specialized in one vertical such as healthcare, legal or home services; firms with proprietary audience data or retail media technology; experiential and event businesses; and promotional products and print distributors joining larger platforms.
