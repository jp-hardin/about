BUSINESS SERVICES · Q4 2026

Managed print and document services. Equipment demand is contracting while the independent dealer channel continues to grow revenue. IDC reported worldwide hardcopy peripheral shipments of 17.2 million units in the second quarter of 2026, down 7.0% year on year (IDC, September 8, 2026). The Cannata Report's 40th Annual Dealer Survey, drawn from 415 dealerships, found that 67% of dealers grew revenue, by 14% on average (The Cannata Report, October 30, 2025), that 47% of dealers now offer managed IT services, and that 73% of those dealers grew the line, by an average of 28.8% (The Cannata Report, managed IT findings). Dan Doyle Jr., chief executive of DEX Imaging, told the publication that dealers need to commit fully to IT, with print representatives supplying the leads and technical specialists closing the sale (The Cannata Report, September 18, 2026).

The buyer pool is concentrated in a small number of acquirers that are moving quickly. DEX Imaging, owned by Staples, completed its sixth acquisition of 2026 in September, UBEO Business Services has announced four, and Pacific Office Automation has closed three, including its entry into Houston, and 45% of surveyed dealers said they planned to acquire a dealership or service company (The Cannata Report, acquisition trends). The manufacturers are consolidating as well: Xerox bought Lexmark for $1.5 billion in 2025 (Pulse 2.0, July 30, 2026), and Ricoh, Toshiba Tec and OKI now manufacture their multifunction devices through one joint venture, ETRIA (Ricoh, February 13, 2025). A dealer's value therefore rests more on its customer contracts, service organization and IT revenue than on the brand it carries, and the terms of its dealer agreements on a change of ownership deserve an early reading. In records and information management, Iron Mountain reported second-quarter 2026 revenue of $2.0 billion, up 18.5% (IT Brief, August 7, 2026).

Testing, inspection and certification. The largest listed companies are growing and acquiring at the same time. SGS reported first-half 2026 sales of CHF 3,683 million, up 7.6%, with organic growth of 5.6% and 14 bolt-on acquisitions completed in 2026 by the date of the release, and it closed its purchase of Applied Technical Services, a North American testing, inspection, calibration and forensics provider, on January 12, 2026 (SGS, July 23, 2026). Bureau Veritas reported first-half organic growth of 5.0% and raised its 2026 guidance to mid- to high-single-digit organic growth (Quartr, Bureau Veritas first-half 2026 results), and UL Solutions grew second-quarter revenue 6.6% organically with an adjusted EBITDA margin of 26.8% (UL Solutions, August 4, 2026).

Private equity is committing capital to testing at every size. In June 2026 the board of Intertek, the UK-listed testing group, unanimously recommended an offer from a company controlled by EQT of £60 in cash per share plus a final dividend of 107.7 pence, which values Intertek at an enterprise value of £10.9 billion and represents a 62% premium to the closing price on April 9, 2026 (Business News Today, June 18, 2026). KPMG records the transaction at $14.6 billion and places it among the largest global private equity deals of the second quarter (KPMG, Pulse of Private Equity Q2 2026). In the United States, TIC Solutions, which was formed in August 2025 when Acuren completed its acquisition of NV5 Global, announced on August 18, 2026 that it had completed three further acquisitions with about $90 million of combined annualized revenue (TIC Solutions, August 18, 2026), and Transcat paid $84 million in cash for Essco Calibration Laboratory in August 2025 (Transcat, August 5, 2025).

Buyers in testing and inspection pay for accreditation, mandated recurring revenue and certified staff. Accreditation and licensure take years to obtain and limit the number of firms that can perform a given test or inspection. Revenue recurs because the work is required by a regulator, an insurer or a customer's quality system, which makes it less sensitive to the economy than project work. Certified technicians and inspectors are scarce, so an acquisition is often the fastest way for a larger group to add capacity in a new region.
