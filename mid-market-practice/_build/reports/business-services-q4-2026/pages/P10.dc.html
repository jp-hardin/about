BUSINESS SERVICES · Q4 2026

Outsourced sales and brokerage. Advantage Solutions, the largest listed sales and marketing agency for consumer packaged goods brands, grew second-quarter 2026 revenue 1.8% to $889.5 million while its adjusted EBITDA fell 12.2%. Revenue in its Branded Services segment fell 20.1%, which management attributed to constrained client spending, insourcing and client losses, and its Experiential segment grew 19.7% (Advantage Solutions, August 5, 2026). Buyers of independent brokers and manufacturers' representatives therefore look closely at the length of each principal relationship and at how much revenue rests on any one brand.

Promotional products. ASI measured North American distributor sales at a record $27.7 billion in 2025, up 4.2%, with nearly 90% of distributors raising prices, by about 11% on average, to offset import costs, and with distributors above $5 million of revenue growing 9.3% (ASI, January 21, 2026). Growth slowed to 1.8% in the first quarter of 2026 and 2.5% in the second, with firms under $1 million of revenue flat and larger firms growing about 4% (ASI Central, July 14, 2026). 4imprint's first-half revenue rose 1% while its operating profit fell 16% on tariff-related supplier costs (4imprint, August 5, 2026), and PPAI's economist described 2025 as a year in which many distributors sold more and made less (PPAI).

Digital strategy and consulting. Gartner forecasts worldwide IT services spending of $1.57 trillion in 2026, up 5.3% after 3.8% growth in 2025 (Gartner, July 27, 2026). Accenture reported fiscal 2026 revenue of $74.2 billion, up 6%, and spent $4.9 billion on acquisitions during the year (Accenture, October 1, 2026), and Cognizant grew second-quarter revenue 4.5% and completed its $634 million acquisition of Astreya (Cognizant, July 29, 2026).

Education technology consulting. The E-Rate program that funds school and library connectivity has a cap of $5.2 billion for the funding year that began on July 1, 2026, against estimated demand of $3.5 billion, and the FCC directed that all eligible requests be funded (FCC, May 11, 2026). The Supreme Court upheld the Universal Service Fund's contribution mechanism in June 2025 (SCOTUSblog), and the FCC ended E-Rate support for hotspot lending and school bus Wi-Fi that September (American Library Association). CoSN's 2026 survey of district technology leaders ranks cybersecurity as the top priority for the eighth year, with 65% reporting inadequate cybersecurity staffing (CoSN via K-12 Dive, May 14, 2026).

Display and signage. Signs of the Times found that 48% of sign companies grew sales in 2025, down from 70% in 2023, that 72% expect growth in 2026, and that recruiting and retaining staff is the leading threat they name (Signs of the Times, March 17, 2026). Daktronics reported sales up 7.1% and a backlog of $311.3 million in its quarter ended August 1, 2026, with commercial orders up 5.3% and live events orders down 48.8% (Daktronics, September 2, 2026), and the exhibition industry's index is forecast to grow 2.1% in 2026 (IAEE, May 4, 2026).

Artificial intelligence is changing what a marketing firm is paid for. The groups under the most pressure are reducing headcount while growth concentrates in technology-led work, and WPP Media sizes generative search advertising at $5.1 billion globally in 2026 (exchange4media, June 16, 2026). Agencies whose revenue depends on producing content or search rankings by the hour should expect buyers to examine how much of that work clients can now do themselves, and agencies with retained client relationships, first-party data and measurable outcomes should expect that examination to work in their favor.
