The buyers in the announced transactions were predominantly operating companies. Of the 73 Benchmark International transactions named in this report, more than half were sold to a company in the seller's own or an adjacent field, including publicly traded acquirers such as Cintas, Bowman Consulting Group, Montrose Environmental Group and Cboe and platforms backed by private equity such as Mod Op, Market Performance Group, Phenna Group and SC Labs. The remainder went to investment firms, search funds and individual buyers, most often where the business was smaller and the owner wanted continuity for staff and customers.

The reasons these buyers gave for acquiring fall into three groups, which are expansion into a new geography, the addition of a service the buyer did not yet offer, and the addition of a business to a platform that an investor already owns. An owner can use the same three reasons to identify likely acquirers, because they point to companies that serve the same customers in a neighboring region, to companies whose clients already ask for the service the owner provides, and to investor-backed platforms that are assembling businesses in the owner's field.
