Preparing for a sale

Sellers who prepare early capture more of the competition for their business

In the second quarter of 2026, 87% of transactions above $5 million attracted three or more offers, and those sales took 11 to 12 months from engagement to closing, yet most sellers had spent less than a year preparing (IBBA and M&A Source Market Pulse). An owner who expects to sell within three years should therefore have reviewed financial statements, a clear record of customer contracts and renewals, and a second layer of management in place before the first buyer conversation.

Higher interest rates make this work more valuable, because lenders are advancing less debt against each dollar of EBITDA and buyers are reserving their strongest offers for businesses whose earnings they can verify quickly. The same preparation helps the accountants, attorneys and wealth advisors who counsel an owner, since tax, estate and reinvestment planning each take longer than the sale process itself.
