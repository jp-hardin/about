Public-market valuation reference

Listed engineering and commercial services companies trade above 12x EBITDA, and marketing companies trade below 9x

Public multiples describe very large companies and are most useful for ranking the segments against one another. Engineering firms carry the highest median at 14.3x, and within commercial and facility services the route-based companies with recurring revenue, such as Cintas at 25.8x and Rollins at 17.9x, trade well above labor-intensive businesses such as ABM at 10.1x and BrightView at 5.9x. Privately held companies in all five segments are priced below these levels, principally because of size, and the GF Data and Market Pulse figures in the deal market section are the more relevant benchmarks.
