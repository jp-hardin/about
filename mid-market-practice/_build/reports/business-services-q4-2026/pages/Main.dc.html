Q4 2026 M&A Sector Report
October 2026 - Jared Hardin | Managing Director | Benchmark International

The quarter in brief

Business services companies enter the fourth quarter of 2026 with acquirers active in all five segments covered in this report and with financing more expensive than at any point this year. Global deal value fell 41% in the third quarter after a record first half, following a Federal Reserve rate increase in September and a 10-year Treasury yield above 5%. The measures closest to founder- and family-owned companies moved the other way: the median multiple on transactions of $5 million to $50 million reached 5.8x in the second quarter, the highest since early 2022, and 87% of transactions above $5 million drew three or more offers.
