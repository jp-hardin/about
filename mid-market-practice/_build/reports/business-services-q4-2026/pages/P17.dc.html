Appraisal management. Appraisal volume depends on mortgage originations, which the Mortgage Bankers Association forecasts at $2.147 trillion for 2026, with the mix shifting toward purchase loans as higher rates reduce refinancing (National Mortgage News, August 21, 2026). Freddie Mac's average 30-year fixed rate was 7.28% on October 1, 2026, compared with 6.34% a year earlier (Freddie Mac). Class Valuation acquired Allstate Appraisal in August (HousingWire, August 18, 2026).

Wealth and financial planning. Fidelity counted 120 transactions among registered investment advisers in the first half of 2026, 9% fewer than a year earlier, while the client assets involved rose 88% to $342.9 billion, and private equity-backed buyers accounted for 107 of the 120 (WealthManagement.com, August 26, 2026). Succession explains much of the supply, because Cerulli reports that 35.3% of advisers expect to retire within ten years and that 27% are unsure of their succession plan (PLANADVISER, August 7, 2026). Dakota recorded 23 wealth management transactions in August 2026 (Dakota).

Trading technology. Broadridge agreed in February 2026 to acquire CQG, a provider of futures and options trading technology, in a transaction that extends its derivatives business (FOW, February 6, 2026).
