BUSINESS SERVICES · Q4 2026

Client spend routed through intermediaries, the effect of AI on junior hiring and unsettled federal rules will each appear in a buyer's diligence. Clients are routing more spend through intermediaries, with global managed service provider spend at $244 billion in 2025 and seven providers controlling more than 70% of it (SIA, September 29, 2026). AI adoption is shifting demand toward experienced workers: a Stanford and King's College London study found junior employment down 3% and senior roles up 6.7% at firms adopting AI (SIA, September 29, 2026). Federal rules remain unsettled on two fronts, with a proposed fee of about $103,000 on new H-1B hires following the court defeat of the 2025 proclamation (SIA, September 8, 2026) and a Department of Labor proposal of February 2026 to replace the independent contractor rule (US Department of Labor).

Acquirers have returned in step with the recovery, and the transactions show what they want: skilled trades and engineering talent tied to construction, specialist healthcare roles, and consulting capability that moves a staffing firm toward project ownership.
