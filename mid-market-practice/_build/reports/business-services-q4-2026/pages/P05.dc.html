Commercial and facility services hold the most active acquirers in business services, because the work is contracted or mandated, it repeats on a route or an inspection schedule, and each acquired branch adds density to a network the buyer already operates. Cintas, which serves more than one million business customers over approximately 12,500 local delivery routes, agreed in March 2026 to acquire UniFirst for about $5.5 billion, and UniFirst's shareholders have approved the transaction (Cintas, fiscal 2026 annual report; American Laundry News, July 6, 2026).

Fire protection and life safety. APi Group reported second-quarter 2026 revenue of $2,254 million, up 13.3% and 10.1% organically, with an adjusted EBITDA margin of 13.8% and a backlog above $5 billion (APi Group, July 30, 2026). APi described CertaSite, a Midwest provider with about $90 million of revenue that it agreed to buy in December 2025, as an "inspection-first" business that moves it toward earning more than 60% of revenue from inspection and service (APi Group, December 10, 2025). Pye-Barker Fire & Safety completed 57 acquisitions in 2025 and a further 22 in the first half of 2026, more than 100 in two years, across fire alarm, sprinkler, suppression and security alarm companies (Security Systems News, March 18, 2026; Pye-Barker, July 30, 2026).

Security services. The SDM 100 ranking of electronic security dealers recorded recurring monthly revenue of $740 million for 2025, up 7%, with 90% of ranked dealers growing that revenue and 76% operating in commercial accounts (SDM Magazine, May 4, 2026). In guard services, Allied Universal closed eight acquisitions between May and July 2026 with about $205 million of combined annual revenue (Allied Universal, September 22, 2026), while Securitas reported flat organic sales in the second quarter (Securitas, July 24, 2026). The Bureau of Labor Statistics counts 1.3 million security guards at a median wage of $18.29 an hour, which makes the wage escalation terms in a guard company's customer contracts a central diligence item (BLS).

Painting and property maintenance. ABM Industries grew revenue 4.2% to $2.3 billion in its fiscal third quarter, with 2.1% organic growth and an equal contribution from acquisitions (ABM, September 8, 2026). FirstService Residential grew 4% on new contract wins (FirstService, July 23, 2026), and BrightView's maintenance revenue rose 1.8% while its adjusted EBITDA fell 15.1% (BrightView, August 4, 2026). Regional platforms are doing much of the buying: 4M Building Solutions has made 16 janitorial acquisitions since January 2023 (Business Wire, July 21, 2026), and Landscape Workshop acquired three commercial landscape maintenance businesses in August 2026 to reach more than 50 locations in 16 states (Utah Business, August 27, 2026). Labor is the common constraint, with 2.4 million janitors and building cleaners earning a median $17.71 an hour (BLS).
