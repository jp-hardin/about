The same survey reports that 60% to 90% of sellers, depending on deal size, had done less than a year of planning before going to market, and that retirement drove between 45% and 72% of sales. McKinsey expects about six million small and mid-sized US businesses to change hands by 2035, and more than half of small-business owners are now over 55 (McKinsey, The great ownership transfer).

Federal tax law is settled for sellers planning a 2026 or 2027 closing. The top long-term capital gains rate remains 20%, and the estate and gift exemption stands at $15 million per person, indexed, under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act of July 2025 (Tax Foundation). The same law raised the qualified small business stock exclusion to $15 million for stock issued after July 4, 2025, restored 100% bonus depreciation and made the 20% pass-through deduction permanent (HBK CPAs & Consultants).
