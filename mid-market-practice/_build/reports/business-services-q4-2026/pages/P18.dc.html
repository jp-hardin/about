Benchmark International in financial and insurance services

Benchmark International has closed three transactions in financial and insurance services since January 2025 and has 10 engagements in progress as of October 1, 2026. New Era Life Insurance Companies bought a Dallas provider of marketing services to insurance companies, and Centri Business Consulting bought a firm that reviews underwriting and claims for insurers and reinsurers. Earlier transactions took two claims adjusting firms to Team One Insurance Services and Davies, two agencies to OneDigital and Acrisure, an appraisal management company to Class Valuation and a trading analytics firm to Cboe.
