TECHNOLOGY · Q4 2026
Public-market valuation reference

Listed medians run from 8.3x EBITDA for IT services to 21.9x for electronics manufacturers, with security software higher still

Public multiples describe companies many times larger than a founder-owned business and are best read as an indication of relative standing among the six focus areas. Electronics manufacturers carry a median of 21.9x on the strength of data center demand, communications and integration companies sit between 15.4x and 16.8x, and government contractors and IT services companies have the lowest medians at 10.5x and 8.3x. The low readings for large IT services firms come as investors weigh how artificial intelligence will change labor-based pricing. Private benchmarks sit below most of these figures. The median US buyout was priced at 12.5x EBITDA in 2025 (PitchBook), completed middle-market transactions in all industries averaged 7.0x in the second quarter of 2026 (GF Data via ACG), and transactions of $5 million to $50 million were priced at 5.8x (IBBA and M&A Source Market Pulse).
