Overview

Positioning Industrial Companies for Maximum Value

Reshoring, automation and supply-chain resilience have put middle-market industrial companies at the top of many buyers' lists. We advise precision manufacturers, fabricators, industrial distributors and service providers, translating machine capability, certifications, engineered-to-order know-how and customer stickiness into the terms strategic and financial buyers use to value them.

Every engagement is led by the Middle Market Team and backed by Benchmark International's global network of strategic acquirers, private equity groups, family offices and independent sponsors.
