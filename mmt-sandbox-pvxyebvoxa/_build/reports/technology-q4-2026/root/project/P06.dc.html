Managed IT & Cloud Services: announced transactions

Platforms, sponsors and public companies are all buying

Prices for managed service providers are almost never published, so the pattern of who is buying is the most useful public evidence. The transactions below show three kinds of acquirer at work: sponsor-backed platforms adding regional providers, sponsors buying the platforms from one another, and public companies buying capacity in cloud and artificial intelligence infrastructure operations.

The published benchmarks point in one direction. Best-in-class providers reached record valuations in 2025 according to the Service Leadership Index (ConnectWise), and acquirers in this focus area pay the most for a high share of revenue under managed service agreements, security services attached to those agreements, low customer concentration and a service desk that runs without the owner.
