Managed service providers came into 2026 with their strongest operating results in several years. Revenue grew 9.6% in 2025 against 7.1% in 2024 and adjusted EBITDA grew 17.1%. Best-in-class providers earned adjusted EBITDA margins above 19% for a sixth consecutive year, and the enterprise value of the average IT solution provider rose about 15% (ConnectWise Service Leadership Index, June 23, 2026).

Growth is harder to find at the level of the individual provider. In Kaseya's survey of more than 1,000 providers, 71% named winning new customers as their main challenge, 24% reported clients cutting IT budgets, and 16% reported difficulty hiring technicians against 9% a year earlier. Almost half ranked artificial intelligence and automation as their clients' leading need, yet only 13% earn meaningful revenue from them (Kaseya 2026 State of the MSP Report).

Cloud demand continues to widen the work available. Cloud infrastructure services revenue rose 43% in the second quarter (Synergy Research Group) and Microsoft reported more than 30 million paid seats of Microsoft 365 Copilot (Microsoft). Vendor licensing changes are also moving customers, and 11:11 Systems bought Ntirety as Broadcom's changes to its VMware partner program reshape how providers operate (ChannelE2E).

“A shift in valuations is visible, as providers with higher shares of managed services achieve superior multiples compared to traditional value-added resellers (VARs).”
Houlihan Lokey, December 2025 IT Services Market Update

Acquirers announced 64 managed service provider deals worldwide in the first quarter of 2026 against 169 in all of 2025, and outside investors led 80%. Evergreen Services Group alone completed 47 acquisitions in 2025 (Omdia). The 20 MSP of Plano, Texas, reached its 49th acquisition in July 2026 (ChannelE2E) and buys providers with $1 million to $5 million of revenue using operating cash flow and bank debt (Omdia).

Sponsors are also trading the platforms themselves. AEA Investors bought a majority of Magna5 from NewSpring Holdings in February 2026 (PrivSource), Berkshire Partners invested in Thrive alongside Court Square in January 2025 (PrivSource), and Park Place Technologies completed its merger with Service Express to form a data center services business with $1.2 billion of annual revenue (Park Place Technologies).

Public companies are buying as well. Cognizant completed its purchase of Astreya in June 2026 and ePlus bought the assets of Daymark Solutions in August (Cognizant; Channel Insider).
